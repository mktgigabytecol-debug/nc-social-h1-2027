Content pillars

GIGABYTE speaks to a productive target — content creators, editors, students of all ages and professionals — without losing our LATAM gaming community.

Entertainment

Culture

Position GIGABYTE within gaming and internet culture — and within the world of creators, students and professionals — not just as a hardware brand.

  • Trends adapted to gaming, tech & productivity
  • Meme-native content
  • Relatable situations: gamers, creators and professionals
  • Fast hook formats (3–5s)

Experience

Product in action

Instead of showing the product, we integrate it into real scenarios — gaming and productive alike (editing, studying, working) — so users understand it through experience, not explanation.

  • POV: “When you…” scenarios
  • Daily gaming, creative and work/study experiences
  • Before vs. after setups
  • Performance moments (FPS, RTX, rendering, multitasking)

Authority

Value & knowledge

Build credibility by helping users get more out of our products — for gaming, content creation, studying and everyday work.

  • Tips & hacks
  • Setup optimization
  • Simple feature explanations
  • “Did you know?” formats

Conversion

Commercial content

Activate demand by connecting content with purchase opportunities and promotions — for gamers and productive users alike.

  • Promotions & campaigns
  • Channel push (“where to buy”)
  • Product highlights with CTA
  • Repurposed high-performing content
